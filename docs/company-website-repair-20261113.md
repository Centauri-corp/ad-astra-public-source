# Company website implementation repair — 13 November 2026

This revision implements the owner's requested change to the public website.
It is a source candidate, not production publication or customer activation.

## What was wrong

The selected rebuild generated a static homepage and explicitly asserted that
the homepage contained no video. Its manifest marked 3D private-only. The neural
interactive scene was reachable on another route, while the film page remained
a storyboard with “PREMIERE COMING SOON.” Tests passing those requirements did
not establish delivery of a cinematic homepage. The production alias was also
serving an older `work` artifact rather than the selected PR36 source.

## What this revision implements

- A visible homepage company introduction explaining the shared intelligence
  direction and how six product families connect to solutions and services.
- An expanded company page with purpose, architecture and operating principles.
- A self-authored folded neural sculpture, loaded only on request, with explicit
  play/pause, reduced-motion handling, off-screen suspension, context-loss
  fallback and disposal. It makes no model, customer, payment or CRM request.
- A rendered twelve-second H.264 motion study, poster, English descriptive track,
  native playback controls, visual description and a homepage navigation link.
  It is silent and does not autoplay. No stock footage or paid generation was used.
- A source provenance record, generator and browser verification script.
- Reconciliation of the public companion pin to the observed PR45 source71e.
  Historical external release receipts are not rewritten or accepted by that pin.

The existing 60-second company-film storyboard is not a finished film. This
revision does not relabel the short study as that premiere, nor claim AGI/ASI,
live brain telemetry, available customer services or supported regions.
