A CLEARER PICTURE

Before you explore.

What can I use today?

You can explore this website, product directions and the clearly labelled workspace design preview. Customer AI access, purchasing and subscriptions are not yet available. See current availability.

Is Ad Astra offering AGI or ASI?

No. AGI is an engineering research direction; ASI is a speculative research horizon. Neither is an achieved or publicly available capability. The artwork is conceptual, not evidence of a deployed intelligence system.

Is Ad Astra available in my country?

Global market positioning is research, not confirmed service coverage. Regional availability requires review and approval. No regions are currently approved for customer service.

Does exploring this design send a prompt or create an account?

No. These story pages do not send prompts, collect microphone audio, create accounts, initiate payments or subscribe you to marketing. Product filters only change which descriptions you see.
