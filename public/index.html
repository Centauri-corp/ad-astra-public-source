What is Ad Astra? +

Ad Astra is an AI-native venture developing Beyond Physics, a Meta-Knowledge Engine intended to connect evidence, reasoning and ideas across disciplines.

Can I use the experiences today? +

The Ad Astra engine is being developed as a public vision. Explore the five guided modes and the markets we are mapping while the live product experience remains in development.

What does Beyond Physics mean? +

It names our ambition to connect knowledge across fields, from science and technology to creativity and learning. It does not claim to surpass established physical laws.

How does human judgment fit in? +

People define the question, evaluate the evidence and decide what comes next. Our aim is to support that process with clearer context and more connected perspectives.
