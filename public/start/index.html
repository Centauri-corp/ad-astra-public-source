YOUR INTENTION

Turn a broad question into a research brief I can check.

THE SHAPE OF A USEFUL OUTCOME

A clearer research starting point

Define the decision before collecting more information. Compare a small number of relevant options, and keep uncertain claims separate from verified evidence.

  1. Write the question, audience and decision deadline in one sentence.
  2. Identify primary sources and record their dates, assumptions and limitations.
  3. Compare the options against agreed criteria; ask a qualified reviewer to check consequential claims.

This sample has not retrieved or verified sources. Specialist work requires appropriate qualified review.

THE SHAPE OF A USEFUL OUTCOME

From a thought to a creative brief

Start with the feeling and the audience. Build one coherent creative direction around a single message, then test whether the idea is clear without an explanation.

  1. Describe the audience, intended feeling and one message to remember.
  2. Choose a visual language, useful references and the formats the work must support.
  3. Review the concept for clarity, accessibility and usage rights before producing final assets.

WRITTEN EXAMPLE / NOT LIVE AIBusiness & Commerce

YOUR INTENTION

Turn a team question into an accountable next step.

THE SHAPE OF A USEFUL OUTCOME

A decision brief your team can review

Make the desired outcome explicit. Compare the options, name the trade-offs and give the next action an owner instead of allowing a draft to become an unreviewed decision.

  1. State the outcome, available resources and constraints.
  2. Compare options using agreed criteria and record the assumptions to validate.
  3. Assign the next action, reviewer and check-in date before changing a business system.

No business records were read and no actions were taken. Financial, legal and operational decisions need appropriate review.

WRITTEN EXAMPLE / NOT LIVE AIDeveloper Platform

YOUR INTENTION

Outline an integration with boundaries I can test.

THE SHAPE OF A USEFUL OUTCOME

An integration that starts with a contract

Define the input and the expected result before selecting a tool. Keep authorization on the server and make retries safe before connecting a production provider.

  1. Document the caller, input schema, permissions and intended response.
  2. Specify validation, tenant boundaries, idempotency and failure behavior.
  3. Test with synthetic identities and data, then review the exact candidate before a controlled release.

An illustrative engineering brief, not deployed code, an API credential or a tested integration.

WRITTEN EXAMPLE / NOT LIVE AIAutonomous Workflows

YOUR INTENTION

Make a repeatable process easier to understand and supervise.

THE SHAPE OF A USEFUL OUTCOME

A workflow with deliberate checkpoints

Separate preparation from execution. Give each step a clear owner and stop condition, and require explicit approval before consequential external actions.

  1. Map the trigger, required inputs and the outcome to prepare.
  2. Name the owner, permitted actions and review checkpoint for each step.
  3. Test failure, retry and stop paths before authorizing any automation.

No workflow is running. The sample does not authorize autonomous actions, outreach or changes to external systems.
