A small plan for understanding

Break a new subject into a practical learning path.

Connect the subject to something familiar. Learn one concept, explain it in your own words, then use a short exercise to find what still needs clarification.

1. Write what you already know and the question you want to answer.
2. Study one foundational concept and explain it with an everyday example.
3. Try an exercise, check the explanation against a trusted source and revise your understanding.
