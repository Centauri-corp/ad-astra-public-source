What this notice covers

This preview explains the website and the current account, text conversation, optional voice and billing designs. It describes known practices while the complete privacy notice is being verified. These trust pages contain no tracking, forms or chat input. Visiting them does not send a question to an AI service.

Delivering a website still involves processing connection information by hosting services. This page does not promise that hosting or service providers retain no data.

What information is used

Account sign-in uses Clerk. Account and sign-in information is processed to verify access; sign-in alone does not establish a paid subscription.

In the current conversation interface, typing does not send your question to the AI service. Text is transmitted when you choose Send. Questions and answers are held for the current interaction; this interface does not save a conversation history for you to return to.

Separate private usage and cost records contain request identifiers and hashes rather than the text of your question. These records support access checks and usage reconciliation. They are distinct from conversation history.

Use only information you have permission to share. Avoid passwords, payment card details and sensitive personal or confidential material in questions.

Your voice stays optional

Voice is optional. Dictation and answer playback use supported on-device speech features only, after you choose them. You can review dictated text before choosing Send. This interface sends no audio to Ad Astra. If on-device speech is unavailable, use text instead.

Speech availability and local processing depend on your browser, device and installed speech features. The page does not start the microphone automatically.

Where providers are involved

Clerk supports account sign-in, Stripe supports hosted billing management, and a configured AI provider processes question text when a permitted request is sent. These providers process the information needed for their part of the service.

Provider processing and retention depend on the applicable service configuration and provider policies. We do not promise zero provider retention or that all processing takes place on your device. The complete provider disclosures are still being verified.

History, retention and requests

A conversation that is not saved as history is different from account, billing, security and usage records. Retention periods, deletion limits and provider handling are still being verified; no fixed retention or deletion deadline is stated here.

The process for privacy, access, export and deletion requests is still being confirmed. A monitored request channel and applicable handling details must be published before new paid access opens. The contact details below do not establish an operating privacy request service.

Review contact details and support availability before sending a request. Do not include identity documents, passwords or conversation content in an initial inquiry.
