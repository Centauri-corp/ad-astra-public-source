Last updated: 23 October 2026. This notice describes the AD ASTRA website at adastra.global and its customer sign-in and AI question features.

Account information

Clerk provides our account and session service. When you create an account, it processes the email address and authentication information needed to verify you and maintain your session.

If you choose Google sign-in, Google shares your account identifier, email address and basic profile information, such as your name and profile image, with our authentication service. We use this information to create or recognize your AD ASTRA account and sign you in. This connection requests identity, email and profile access. It does not request access to your Gmail messages, Drive files, contacts or calendar.

We do not sell Google account information or use it for advertising. We do not include your Google profile or email address in the AI question sent to our model provider.

Questions and AI responses

When the AI engine is available and you submit a question, we send your question and selected mode to OpenAI to generate a response. AD ASTRA does not save chat history in this application. This does not mean that providers retain no data: their security, abuse-prevention and retention policies apply. Avoid entering information you do not want processed by these services.

Website operation and security

Vercel hosts the website and API. Hosting and authentication services process technical request information, including network information, to deliver and protect the service. Our application logs request identifiers, response status, timing and a general outcome; it does not intentionally log questions, answers or session tokens.

Redis stores a hashed account identifier, quota counters and budget reservations so that usage limits work across requests. These counters expire automatically. The application requires authentication before reserving usage or sending an AI question.

Cookies and service providers

Sign-in uses cookies or similar browser storage to keep your session working. Google, Clerk, Vercel and OpenAI may process information in countries outside your own under their applicable policies. Read their notices for details: Google, Clerk, Vercel and OpenAI.

Your choices and requests

You can sign out, choose another available sign-in method, or remove Google's connection to AD ASTRA. Removing that connection does not automatically delete your AD ASTRA account.

For questions or requests to access, correct or delete your AD ASTRA account information, contact zohebkhanfiji@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting. Service providers may retain records needed for security, legal obligations or their stated retention policies.

We will update this page when the service's data practices change.
