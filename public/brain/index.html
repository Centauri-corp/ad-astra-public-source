WEBSITE VISUAL ARCHITECTURE / 01—04

The customer journey
stays legible.

Each layer gives the visitor a clear answer to one question: what am I trying to do, what supports it, who decides, and what is available now?

  1. 01 / Intent

    Define the question.

    Start with the human goal, context and desired next step.

  2. 02 / Evidence

    Show the support.

    Connect ideas to sources, uncertainty and alternative explanations.

  3. 03 / Reasoning

    Make the path visible.

    Organize analysis, planning and creation without hiding the limits.

  4. 04 / Oversight

    Keep people in control.

    Permission, review, budget and release status govern consequential actions.

SIX STATES / PRODUCT LANGUAGE

One visual system.
Six ways forward.

The existing neural states become a shared language for the home page, intelligence vision and future customer workspace.

01 / STATEAnalyzing

Frame the question, separate signal from noise and identify what needs evidence.

02 / STATEReasoning

Connect claims and perspectives with a traceable, inspectable path.

03 / STATEPlanning

Turn understanding into options, dependencies and a considered next step.

04 / STATEActing

Prepare action only when permissions, boundaries and human approval are present.

05 / STATELearning

Revisit assumptions, compare outcomes and carry forward useful context.

06 / STATEAligning

Keep intent, evidence, oversight and availability aligned before release.
