THE QUESTION

How could a new idea become a useful research project?

01 / INTENT

Make the question specific.

Name the decision the research should support. Define the audience, scope and what a useful outcome would look like.

  • Write one bounded question.
  • Record the context and constraints.
  • Separate the desired outcome from assumptions.

This example contains no research findings.

STEP 01 OF 04

HUMAN-DIRECTED BY DESIGN

Four layers.
One clear boundary.

An ambitious experience should make its limits easier to understand, not harder to see.

  1. 01 / INTENT

    Start with you.

    Your goal and context frame the work. The interface should never substitute its own objective.

  2. 02 / EVIDENCE

    Keep sources close.

    Show what supports an idea, what conflicts and what remains unknown.

  3. 03 / REASONING

    Make it inspectable.

    Present explanations, alternatives and assumptions for review — not a claim to reveal hidden model reasoning.

  4. 04 / OVERSIGHT

    People decide.

    Permissions and human approval govern consequential actions. This page performs none.
