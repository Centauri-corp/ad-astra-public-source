Language and context

Countries, languages, communities, and professional practices differ within every continent. Workspace content must adapt to the relevant locale and approved customer context.

02

Qualification before availability

Country and jurisdiction records must establish provider availability, privacy, data handling, language, support, billing and tax where relevant, and a reviewed release decision.

Ask, Connect, Create, and Decide stay familiar. Local reading direction, dates, numbers, currencies, sources, and support arrangements require their own validation.
