Understand the current boundary

This page describes controls in the current service design. It is not a security certification, audit report or guarantee that every hosted flow has been accepted for customer use. Check product availability before relying on a service.

The website and workspace designs are previews. Customer AI access and new paid subscriptions are not opened by these pages. Product availability and service regions must be confirmed before purchase.

Account and workspace access

Account sign-in uses Clerk. Account and sign-in information is processed to verify access; sign-in alone does not establish a paid subscription.

Before a model request can proceed, the service checks the signed-in account, workspace membership and subscription access. Customer access remains subject to availability and service limits.

A visible page or selected plan does not grant access. Requests that cannot establish permission or available capacity are denied. Protect your account and review unexpected account or billing activity.

Questions and usage records

In the current conversation interface, typing does not send your question to the AI service. Text is transmitted when you choose Send. Questions and answers are held for the current interaction; this interface does not save a conversation history for you to return to.

Separate private usage and cost records contain request identifiers and hashes rather than the text of your question. These records support access checks and usage reconciliation. They are distinct from conversation history.

The AI provider must process the question to produce an answer. Private usage records without question text do not imply that providers retain no data. See the privacy notice for the current disclosure and unresolved retention details.

Voice and hosted billing

Voice is optional. Dictation and answer playback use supported on-device speech features only, after you choose them. You can review dictated text before choosing Send. This interface sends no audio to Ad Astra. If on-device speech is unavailable, use text instead.

Existing customers can start from account billing to review their account and, where available, open Stripe-hosted billing management. Account access and billing management depend on a verified sign-in and the availability of the connected service.

Use account billing to reach the hosted management flow. Never put card details or account credentials into a question or a security report.

If you notice a security concern

Do not attempt to access another person’s account or data. Avoid sharing credentials, private records or a full conversation when describing a concern. A useful initial report identifies the affected page, approximate time and a short description of what happened.
