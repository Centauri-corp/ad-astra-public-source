A future source-backed record of AD ASTRA releases, research milestones and product changes.

Meaningful progress, not hype

Updates should distinguish a design concept, an engineering prototype, a passed test, an approved launch and a delivered customer capability. Provide exact dates and links to authoritative evidence when appropriate.

Release notes people can use

Explain what changed, who it affects, availability by environment, known limitations, compatibility and next steps. Avoid generic assertions of 24/7 autonomy or achieved scientific breakthroughs without operational proof.

Editorial controls

Every story needs an owner, fact check, rights review, publishing date and correction process. No artificial backdated launches or invented partnerships should enter this feed.
