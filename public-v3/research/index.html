An evidence-first approach to exploring scientific questions, practical challenges and emerging possibilities.

Begin with the real question

A research brief should identify the hypothesis, scope, timeframe and what would count as convincing evidence. It should distinguish primary sources, literature synthesis, experimental data and speculative proposals.

Connect without overstating

Interdisciplinary discovery can join physics, computing, energy, biology, engineering and human learning. Connections remain hypotheses until tested, and research summaries should contain publication dates, limitations and alternative explanations.

From exploration to review

The intended workflow is question, source retrieval, provenance check, synthesis, uncertainty map, independent review and a next-step plan. This page describes the method under development, not a currently certified research service.
