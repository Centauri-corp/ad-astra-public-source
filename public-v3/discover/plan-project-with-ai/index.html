Plan a Project With AI Without Losing Control

Turn a broad idea into milestones, task owners, dependencies and practical review steps.

Human-directed · In development

Turn a broad idea into milestones, task owners, dependencies and practical review steps.

Define success

Write the end state, deadline if known, budget limit and important constraints. A useful plan must be specific enough to check against these requirements.

Divide into milestones

Ask for a small number of meaningful milestones, each with a clear deliverable. Avoid converting every possible activity into an immediate priority.

Find dependencies

For each milestone, note prerequisites, external decisions, and tasks that cannot begin until something else is completed.

Assign responsibility

Make owners and approval requirements explicit. AI can help draft a plan, but it cannot grant approvals or verify that another person accepted a task.

Review risks and progress

Use a short recurring review of completed work, blockers, changed assumptions and next decisions. Update the plan when evidence changes, not just because a calendar date passed.

AD ASTRA is developing an informational, human-directed intelligence experience. Conceptual diagrams are not operational AI systems or substitute for expert review.
