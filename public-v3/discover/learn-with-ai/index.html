How to Learn a New Topic With AI

Use explanations, questions and independent checks to support more effective self-study.

Human-directed · In development

Use explanations, questions and independent checks to support more effective self-study.

Choose a precise learning objective

Instead of 'teach me biology,' ask to explain one concept at your current level. Specify whether you want a simple overview, worked example or detailed treatment.

Ask for progressive explanations

Start with a short explanation, then ask what assumptions it relies on and what common misunderstandings to watch for. Request a second example rather than merely asking the AI to repeat itself.

Practice recall

After an explanation, close the answer and try to explain the concept in your own words. Ask for practice questions and check answers against reliable learning material.

Identify gaps

Keep a short list of terms you cannot define or problems you cannot solve. Revisit those gaps before moving to a harder concept.

Verify facts and maintain agency

AI-generated explanations can be wrong. Use textbooks, original sources, teachers or other reliable materials to verify important points, particularly when accuracy matters.
