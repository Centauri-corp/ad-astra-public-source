# AD ASTRA — Intelligence Engine, Products & Public Solutions
Status: proposed structure. Only independently verified, gated existing workflows may be described as available. No newly listed product is activated by this catalog.

## Intelligence Engine
1. Experience: public web, customer, developer and enterprise clients.
2. Identity: verified auth, organizations, tenant membership, entitlements.
3. Model gateway: versioned model allowlist, quotas, spending reservations, request validation, fail-closed security gates.
4. Reasoning and knowledge: scoped context, retrieval, citation provenance, evaluations, tool constraints.
5. Durable agents: queue-based tasks, worker identity, idempotency, execution audit and separate human approvals for external actions.
6. Business services: CRM, billing, provisioning and support via signed, tenant-scoped event contracts.
7. Research: separate restricted AI/AGI/ASI evaluation environments; no unverified customer autonomy claims.
8. Assurance: telemetry, incidents, release evidence, model safety, privacy and cost reconciliation.

## Product and solution ownership
Canonical proposal: `intelligence/public-product-catalog.json`. Product owners must approve scope and assertions before publishing. Every item has explicit status, owner division and pricing state.
